# References

Curated sources for tracing, context management, and failure analysis features.

## Context management

- Mei, L. et al. (2025). *A Survey of Context Engineering for Large Language Models.* [arXiv:2507.13334](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.13334)
- Hu, Y. et al. (2026). *Memory for Autonomous LLM Agents: Mechanisms, Evaluation, and Emerging Frontiers.* [arXiv:2603.07670](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07670) — formalises agent memory as a write–manage–read loop; our strategies map onto context-resident compression and hierarchical virtual context.
- Lindenbauer, T. et al. (2025). *The Complexity Trap: Simple Observation Masking Is as Efficient as LLM Summarization for Agent Context Management.* [arXiv:2508.21433](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21433) — basis for default observation masking.
- Sun, W. et al. (2025). *Scaling Long-Horizon LLM Agent via Context-Folding.* [arXiv:2510.11967](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11967) — basis for `ContextFoldingStrategy`.
- Anthropic (2025). *Effective Context Engineering for AI Agents.* https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents

## Tracing and evaluation

- Yehudai, A. et al. (2025/2026). *Survey on Evaluation of LLM-based Agents.* [arXiv:2503.16416](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16416)
- OpenTelemetry. *Semantic Conventions for Generative AI.* https://opentelemetry.io/docs/specs/semconv/gen-ai/
- Anthropic (2025). *How We Built Our Multi-Agent Research System.* https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system

## Failure analysis

- Cemri, M. et al. (2025). *Why Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Fail?* [arXiv:2503.13657](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13657), NeurIPS 2025 — **MAST** (14 modes / 3 categories). Primary taxonomy source.
- Dao, M.-D. et al. (2026). *Agentic Design Patterns: A System-Theoretic Framework.* [arXiv:2601.19752](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19752)
- Zhu, K. et al. (2025). *Where LLM Agents Fail and How They Can Learn From Failures.* arXiv preprint.

## General background

- *Agentic AI: A Comprehensive Survey of Architectures, Applications, and Future Directions.* [arXiv:2510.25445](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25445)
- *Agentic AI: Architectures, Taxonomies, and Evaluation of LLM Agents.* [arXiv:2601.12560](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12560)
